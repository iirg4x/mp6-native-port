# Mario Party 6 Native Port v0.4.0

Changes since v0.3.0.

## Gameplay and stability

- Added the end-of-match results ceremony, rankings, statistics, saving and return to the menus.
- Unavailable minigames now skip without freezing board progression. Skipped minigames do not award coins.
- Fixed crashes when using, placing or triggering Orbs, including Mushroom effects and space traps.
- Fixed black screens during Towering Treetop's bridge, slide and spring sequences.
- Fixed the nighttime Last Five Turns crash and a DK dice sequence that could get stuck.
- Fixed pressing Start after the opening movie had replayed.
- Fixed DK's scared reaction repeating instead of playing once.

## Graphics

- Added optional ambient occlusion for contact shading, with Off, Subtle and Strong settings that change during play.
- Improved Towering Treetop's scenery grounding with AO enabled, including paths, logs and slide-area terrain.
- Protected board-space artwork, foreground foliage and Towering Treetop's water from unwanted AO shading.
- Corrected Boo's transparency and distorted, oversized or incorrectly fading capsule particles.
- Improved widescreen shop descriptions, Boo-house transitions and results screens, including the full theater backdrop.
- Fixed objects and interface elements disappearing while their graphics first load. Brief first-use hitches can still occur.
- Optimized rendering and AO processing on Android and Windows; performance gains vary by device and settings.

## Controls and usability

- Added a complete customizable Android touch controller: both sticks, D-pad and all GameCube buttons, with movable controls, sizing, opacity and visibility settings.
- Fixed Android fullscreen and removed desktop-only window options.
- Simplified settings descriptions, reduced unnecessary restart requirements and moved Fast Forward into Mods.
- Fixed console closing, clearing and command suggestions; added GPU timing information where supported.
- Windows releases now open without a separate terminal window.

## Updating and known limitations

Older quick save states require their matching build; normal in-game saves remain supported.
Minigames are still unavailable, and the reported persistent sound loop remains unresolved.

## Downloads

- **Windows:** download the `win-x64.zip`, extract it into a new folder, and run `mp6native.exe`.
- **Android:** install the `android-arm64.apk` (Android 9 or newer, 64-bit ARM).
- Both packages require your own Mario Party 6 (USA) disc image. No game assets are included.
- Back up your normal in-game saves before updating. Do not uninstall the Android app to update it.
- `SHA256SUMS.txt` contains checksums for the downloads.
