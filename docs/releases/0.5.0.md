# Starlit Party v0.5.0

Starlit Party is a native port of Mario Party 6 (formerly "Mario Party 6 Native Port").

Changes since v0.4.0, which had Party Mode on Towering Treetop only.

## New boards, modes and minigames

- **All six Party Mode boards.** E. Gadd's Garage, Faire Square, Snowflake
  Lake, Castaway Bay and Clockwork Castle join Towering Treetop.
- **Minigames.** 49 of the game's 81 minigames are playable, with their
  instruction screens and results. Boards play them between turns like the
  original. The rest, including the three Bowser minigames, are not available
  yet and are skipped with an on-screen notice.
- **More of the game is open:** Solo Mode, Mini-Game Mode and its game
  variants, Mic Mode, the Star Bank, the Option menu, and the opening movie,
  ending and credits.
- **Microphone support:** the Mic games can be played with your own voice on
  PC, Android and the web. The game asks for microphone access when a Mic game
  needs it.
- All of it supports widescreen.

## New: play in the browser and on iPhone

- The game runs in a web browser as an installable app. It reads your own
  disc image on the device, keeps saves and settings in the browser, and works
  offline once installed.
- iPhone: touch controller and a full-screen layout that keeps clear of the
  gesture bar.
- Desktop browsers support keyboard, mouse and controllers, including rumble.

## Mods

- **Quick Party:** faster boards, text and intro skips, and Party Mode opens
  straight on board selection. Your own board events, countdowns and
  button-mash events keep normal speed, and lines that state the stakes, like
  Bowser's penalty, wait for your button press. Computer players stay fast.
- **Unlock All:** unlocks everything without touching your records or coins.

## Frame rate and loading

- Unlocked FPS with three modes: Off, Responsive and Smooth. Smooth, the
  default, shows evenly paced frames at your display's refresh rate.
- The first launch builds shaders behind a progress screen, so scenes no
  longer stutter the first time they appear.
- Faster loading, and music no longer hitches when a track starts.
- Lower memory use on every platform.

## Graphics

- Ambient occlusion now works on phones and in the browser, and lowers its
  cost automatically when the frame rate drops.
- New die icon, and the port has a new name: Starlit Party.

## Controls and usability

- New launcher and settings menus.
- Full keyboard and mouse controls on Windows.
- Movies can be imported with the game files, and the launcher offers to
  re-link older imports so minigame previews play.

## Fixes

- Saves are written safely, so an interrupted write can no longer empty a
  memory card file.

## Updating and known limitations

- The first launch after updating builds shaders once.
- Older quick save states need their matching build. Normal in-game saves
  remain supported.

## Downloads

- **Windows:** download the `win-x64.zip`, extract it into a new folder, and
  run `mp6native.exe`.
- **Android:** install the `android-arm64.apk` (Android 9 or newer, 64-bit
  ARM).
- **iPhone and browsers:** open the web app and add it to your home screen.
- All versions require your own Mario Party 6 (USA) disc image. No game
  assets are included. Back up your in-game saves before updating.
